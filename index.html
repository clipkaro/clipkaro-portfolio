Services

Included with every Short

Each engagement is delivered as a finished, publish-ready file. You provide one long-form video; we handle the complete edit.

Hook-led opening

The strongest moment opens the Short, holding attention through the first two seconds and through to the payoff.

Silence & filler removal

Dead air, pauses, and digressions are removed for tight, consistent pacing from start to finish.

Animated captions

Bold, centred captions animated word by word, with the key word highlighted — the format used by leading Shorts channels.

Zooms & punch-ins

Timed zooms and cuts that keep the frame dynamic and the energy consistent throughout.

Sound design

Whooshes, hits, and background music placed precisely against the beats of the clip.

Colour, audio & metadata

Polished colour and levelled, clear audio — delivered with a ready-to-publish title and caption for every Short.
